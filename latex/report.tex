{\large\bfseries Report}\\[2pt]
{\small A pre-registered \emph{in silico} falsification programme on a redox-switch model
of parvalbumin-interneuron damage}\\[2pt]
{\small Nikita Filippov \textperiodcentered\
\href{https://github.com/stockman2/SchizoStudy-v5}{github.com/stockman2/SchizoStudy-v5}
\textperiodcentered\ 24 October 2026}
\end{center}
\vspace{-4pt}

\noindent\textbf{Abstract.} We built a mechanistic ODE model of oxidative damage to
parvalbumin (PV) interneurons --- a bistable peroxiredoxin/sulfiredoxin redox switch, a
two-stage inducible defence ($\tau_m=12$\,h, $\tau_s=63$\,h) with priming, asymmetry, and
per-cell capacity, a Phillips--Robinson sleep--wake switch, and a Wilson--Cowan cortex
closing the loop through disinhibition --- and spent most of the effort trying to break
it. Every hypothesis was written as a numbered rule; every threshold was derived from
expectations and variances before the computation; verdicts sit in an append-only
ledger and are never revised. Of 128 pre-registered parts across 26 rules, 66 passed,
48 failed, 13 were unfalsifiable, one fell outside its own analytic bracket. The model
describes the generation and termination of an episode under oxidative load; the
failures are the main result.

\section{Object of study}\label{sec:intro}
\emph{Hypothesis.} PV interneurons hold cortical excitation in check, set the gamma
rhythm, and pay with the highest energy turnover in the network, hence a large peroxide
flux. Part of the antioxidant defence is produced only while the NMDA receptor --- the
cell's activity sensor --- works; a weak receptor means less defence and more oxidants,
and oxidants suppress the receptor: a positive loop. The overloaded PV cell does not die
but \emph{silences} (less parvalbumin, less GAD67), the cortex disinhibits, load rises:
a second loop; a disinhibited hippocampus raises dopamine responsivity (the Grace
circuit, in which hippocampal output drives the dopamine system) --- the bridge to
positive symptoms. Each arrow is supported experimentally; the chain as a
whole never has been. The quantitative question: is the loop gain large enough to
\emph{close}, i.e.\ to make the cell bistable? Bistability is what converts slow
accumulation into a sharp transition with hysteresis --- the clinical shape (long
prodrome, abrupt first episode, episodes of hours--days).

\emph{Strategy.} Build the minimal model; then falsify it systematically: numbered rules
with parts, thresholds from analytic expectations only, append-only verdict ledger,
bitwise-verified code, frozen reference implementation.

\section{The model}\label{sec:model}
The model is a stack of four layers with separated scales: the cell (minutes--days),
the sleep switch (hours), the cortex (adiabatic), the population (via one scalar).

\subsection{Redox core}\label{sec:core}
Let $x\in[0,1]$ be the working fraction of peroxiredoxin, $c$ the receptor-maintained
protection, $u$ the peroxide, $\nu$ the load. $u$ is fast (algebraic balance); the
dynamics are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:core}
\begin{aligned}
&\nu=\varepsilon u+\frac{xu}{1+u},\qquad
\varepsilon=e_0c^{\gamma},\qquad
N=\frac{N_0}{1+u/K_N},\\
&\dot x=c^{\eta}(1-x)-\mu_0\frac{xu^2}{1+u},\qquad
\dot c=\frac{1}{T}\Bigl(N+\frac{\beta u}{K_B+u}-c\Bigr).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The first line and the $x$-equation are measured kinetics (Selvaggio et al.\ 2018); the
$c$-equation is an assumption of form. The core is a two-variable reduction of the
Selvaggio--Coelho--Salvador model \cite{selvaggio}: the thioredoxin system is in excess
(so it collapses into an effective repair rate), peroxide is adiabatically eliminated,
and the slow sulfiredoxin induction becomes $c$. Concretely: the peroxide ODE of the full
kinetics, $\dot u=\nu-\varepsilon u-xu/(1+u)$, is collapsed by time-scale separation
($u$ relaxes in seconds) into the algebraic balance $\nu=\varepsilon u+xu/(1+u)$ ---
production equals clearance. This balance is a quadratic in $u$, so at each instant the
load $\nu$ (the model's input, not a state variable) fixes $u$ implicitly, and $u$ is
the only channel through which $\nu$ acts on $x$ and $c$. In the simulation the per-cell
load is $\nu=L(t)\,v_j$, with $L(t)$ the set-level load process of
section~\ref{sec:gap} and $v_j$ the cell multiplier of section~\ref{sec:pop}. Notation:
\begin{itemize}
\item $e_0$: scale of the non-enzymatic clearance $\varepsilon u$; $\mu_0$: scale of
the two-hit hyperoxidation $\mu_0 xu^2/(1+u)$ relative to repair;
\item $\eta$: exponent of the defence-amplified repair $c^\eta(1-x)$; $\gamma$: exponent of
the bypass clearance $\varepsilon=e_0c^\gamma$;
\item $K_N$: saturation of the peroxide suppression of the receptor-maintained defence
$N=N_0/(1+u/K_N)$, with $N_0<1$ a congenitally weak receptor;
\item $\beta$, $K_B$: strength and half-saturation of the stress induction
$\beta u/(K_B+u)$;
\item $T$: defence-protein lifetime in repair-time units.
\end{itemize}
The provenance of each term is documented in the repository.

\begin{proposition}[Bistability]
Over-oxidised peroxiredoxin is repaired slowly, so working enzyme \emph{decreases} with
peroxide while clearance \emph{requires} enzyme: the feedback in~\eqref{eq:core}
produces two saddle-node folds and a hysteresis window of loads in which ``defence on''
and ``defence off'' are both stable. Bistability of the fast layer (defence $c$ frozen)
is exact: with $\mu=\mu_0/c^{\eta}$ the nullcline
$\nu(u)=\varepsilon u+u/(1+u+\mu u^2)$ is non-monotone iff
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi}
\varepsilon<\Phi(\mu),\qquad
\Phi(\mu)=\max_{u>0}\frac{\mu u^2-1}{(1+u+\mu u^2)^2},
\end{equation}
since $\tfrac{d\nu}{du}=\varepsilon+(1-\mu u^2)/(1+u+\mu u^2)^2$; the folds are the
extrema of $\nu(u)$, and the slow variable $c$ moves them without creating or removing
the window. Measured cells (the 15 cell lines of Selvaggio et al.~\cite{selvaggio})
lie outside the window; entering it requires repair weakened $1.6$--$22$-fold --- the
proposed state of PV cells under weak NMDA signalling.
\end{proposition}


\emph{Parameter sets.} Nine parameters are sampled ($e_0$, $\mu_0$, $K_N$, $K_B$, $T$
log-uniform; $\eta\in[1,2]$, $\gamma\in[0,1]$, $N_0\in[0.3,1]$ uniform) with
$\beta=N_0\cdot U(0,3)$ (measured induction up to $2.85\times$). A set is accepted iff
bistable: window ratio $>1.05$ and two stable states at the geometric-mean load.
These accepted sets are the model's \emph{potential patients}: only a set whose cell
already sits in the bistable regime --- the weakened-defence state the hypothesis
postulates for PV cells under weak NMDA signalling --- can develop the switch-mediated
damage the model describes; everything else in the sampled space is healthy by
construction and is not studied.
$K=200{,}000$ candidates $\to$ $n_{\mathrm{sel}}=2400$ accepted sets (seeded, cached);
the resting load sits at $q=0.10$ of the window.

\subsection{Inducible defence}\label{sec:defence}
\emph{Two stages.} Induction signal $m$, then protein $s$, added to $c$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:twostage}
\dot m=\frac{1}{\tau_m}\Bigl(\beta\frac{u}{K_B+u}-m\Bigr),\qquad
\dot s=\frac{1}{\tau_s}(m-s),\qquad
\tau_m=12\,\mathrm{h},\ \tau_s=63\,\mathrm{h},
\end{equation}
with a step response that is always sigmoidal; half-level at 57\,h. The stationary
state (hence window, folds, gap law) is unchanged; only the transient changes.

\emph{Priming.} A trace $p$ on the induction signal amplifies it:
$\dot p=(u/(K_B+u)-p)/\tau_p$, $\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}=\beta(1+Ap)$, $A=2$ (ceiling
$\times3$, measured; $\tau_p$ scanned --- unmeasured).

\emph{Asymmetry.} Rise and decay constants of $s$ are split, $r=\tau_{\downarrow}/\tau_{\uparrow}$.

\begin{proposition}[Asymmetric integrator]\label{prop:wr}
For a two-level daily input, high for a fraction $f$ of the day, the stationary mean of
the integrating variable stands at
\begin{equation}\label{eq:wr}
w(r)=\frac{fr}{fr+1-f},
\end{equation}
$f=0.705$ (measured wake fraction). Asymmetry turns the integrator into a peak detector
($r>1$) or trough detector ($r<1$); no fitted numbers enter~\eqref{eq:wr}.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Capacity loop.} Per cell, while damaged, $N_0\to N_0(1-a_n)$ (loss 1\,d), recovery
slow (30\,d): a latch on recovery, not on load. \emph{Dopamine loop}: a tone $d$
driven by disinhibition $E/E_r-1$ (excitation above its resting level $E_r$) or by the
damaged fraction $D$ ($\tau_d=7$\,d; $D$ is defined in section~\ref{sec:gap}) multiplies
the load by
$(1+a_d d)$; at $a_d=0$ the model is reproduced bitwise.

\subsection{Sleep--wake switch}\label{sec:sleep}
Phillips--Robinson, ``new nominal'' set (Skeldon et al.\ 2014), taken whole, untuned:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pr}
\begin{aligned}
&Q(V)=\frac{Q_{\max}}{1+e^{-(V-\theta)/\sigma_Q}},\quad
\tau_V\dot V_v=\nu_{vm}Q(V_m)+D_v-V_v,\quad
\tau_M\dot V_m=A_m+\nu_{mv}Q(V_v)-V_m,\\
&D_v=B_0+\nu_{vc}\cos\tfrac{2\pi(t-\alpha)}{24}+A_G G-k_d D_{\mathrm{cpl}},\quad
\dot G=\begin{cases}(1-G)/18.2\,\mathrm{h}&\text{awake},\\-G/4.2\,\mathrm{h}&\text{asleep},\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
wakefulness $:=\;Q(V_m)>1$\,s$^{-1}$. Here $Q$ is the firing-rate sigmoid
($Q_{\max}=100$\,s$^{-1}$, threshold $\theta$, slope $\sigma_Q$); $V_v$, $V_m$ the
sleep- and monoaminergic-nucleus voltages with time constants $\tau_V$, $\tau_M$ and mutual
inhibition $\nu_{vm}$, $\nu_{mv}$;
$D_v$ the drive to the sleep nucleus --- constant part $B_0$, circadian amplitude
$\nu_{vc}$ with phase $\alpha$, somnogen weight $A_G$; $G$ the somnogens;
$-k_d D_{\mathrm{cpl}}$ the sleep--damage coupling, with $D_{\mathrm{cpl}}$ the mean
damage or the damaged fraction. Checks against publication: folds at
$1.450/2.463$\,mV; free cycle $15.5/8.5/24.0$\,h; somnogen swing
$0.527$ vs.\ $0.50$ --- unfitted. Damage enters via $-k_d D_{\mathrm{cpl}}$
($k_d=2$\,mV): a damaged population suppresses the sleep nucleus and lengthens
wakefulness.

\subsection{Cortex}\label{sec:cortex}
Wilson--Cowan pair ($E$ excitatory, $I$ PV), with gain $a$ and threshold $\theta$ suppressed
in the notation, $F(z)=1/(1+e^{-a(z-\theta)})$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:wc}
\dot E=-E+F(c_1E-c_2gI+P_e),\qquad
\dot I=-I+F(c_3E-c_4gI),\qquad
g=1-0.4\,D,
\end{equation}
$P_e=P_{e0}+P_{e1}\min(Q(V_m)/Q_{\mathrm{wake}},1)$; here $c_1$--$c_4$ are the
connectivity weights, $P_e$ the wakefulness-borne drive to $E$, $Q_{\mathrm{wake}}$ its
wake threshold. The multiplier on \emph{all} PV
output weights with $g_{\mathrm{dep}}=0.4$ is from Volman et al.\ 2011 (inhibition to
60\,\%). Cortical scales are $10^3$ times faster than everything else: stationary
solution by damped iteration (adiabatic elimination); the gamma rhythm is absent by
design. At full damage $E$ rises 19\,\% --- a consequence of two literature numbers,
not a fitted gain.

\subsection{Population}\label{sec:pop}
$J=32$ cells differing in one scalar, the load multiplier
$v_j=\exp(\sigma_v z_{p_j})/\mathrm{mean}$: $\sigma_v=0.10$ is the spread and $z_{p_j}$
the fixed normal quantiles of the edge-pinned probability grid (pinned to $J=32$).
Cells couple only through network activity (load on survivors
rises with the damaged fraction). Gradualness is \emph{from the spread, not the
network} (F5, refuted). The grid subtlety: $J$ is a model parameter through the
realised spread, $\mathrm{std}(\ln v)=0.1226/0.1060/0.0980/0.0943$ at
$J=8/16/32/64$ vs.\ $0.0910$ in the limit ($O(1/J)$ convergence), with log-sensitivity
$\approx2.5$ (section~\ref{sec:J}).

\subsection{Load, episodes, and the gap law}\label{sec:gap}
The load $L(t)$: base at $q=0.10$ of the window; circadian swing 20\,\% --- the weight
$A_H$ of the somnogen deviation $G-\bar G$ in the load, calibrated on the switch;
fast noise $\sigma=0.08$ on log-load, correlation $0.3T$;
slow noise ($\sigma_s$) \emph{off} --- withdrawn in F6, F18, but $\sigma_s$ stays in
the band budget; optional serotonin bursts
($\times1.5$, rate $0.1$/day, decay 2\,h, ceiling $\times2$); auxiliary protocols for
the loop rules (two-phase break--release; static multiplier $n_{\mathrm{mul}}$ as the
loop's static equivalent).

\begin{definition}[Episode, band]
An episode is the time with damaged fraction $D>1/2$ (the continuous mean-damage
variable for cross-$J$ comparisons; crossing moment interpolated inside the step). The
\emph{band} is the set of parameter sets whose upward gap is covered by the available
burst and whose downward gap fits the noise budget $2\sigma+\sigma_s$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Gap conservation]\label{prop:gap}
With window ratio $W$, load position $q$ (log scale), feedback factor $\kappa$, daily
peak and trough $P_+,P_-$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gap}
\ln\frac{W^{1-q}}{P_+}+\ln(W^q\kappa P_-)=\ln\frac{W\kappa P_-}{P_+}\quad(q\text{-independent}).
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
Typical sets: sum $\approx9\,\sigma$ of load noise; the clinical picture (rare episodes
of hours--days) needs $\approx4.5\,\sigma$. \emph{The same noise cannot both break
rarely and release quickly.} The exits: a one-sided trigger, or a variable defence that
raises its own lower boundary --- the pursued route (section~\ref{sec:defence}).

\section{Computation}\label{sec:code}
The computation is organised around the rules: each hypothesis is one rule file, blocks
are requested through shared constructors and hash-deduplicated (78 requested $\to$ 40
unique), noise is drawn per block so blocks are strictly paired, and every part records
its verdict in an append-only ledger (date and block hashes) --- verdicts are never
revised; a defect is annotated beside the verdict and re-asked as a new rule on a fresh
seed. Run commands and the file layout live in the repository README (linked in the
title).

\subsection{Statistics}\label{sec:stats}
A criterion is usable only if its null value, its noise at our sample size, and its
power are known before the run:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mde}
\mathrm{MDE}=(z_{0.95}+z_{0.80})\,\mathrm{SE}=2.4865\,\mathrm{SE};
\end{equation}
agreement claims use a $3\,\mathrm{SE}$ tolerance that must exceed the MDE, or the
claim is unfalsifiable in principle. The sign test carries a \emph{mandatory direction};
medians use the one-sided
order-statistic bound; gaps use within-set CV$^2$ (null exactly one); every criterion
self-tests on synthetic data with known answers before any run. The analytic risk model
(one model for all rules): the log-load sits at $m_0=\ln(\mathrm{lo}\cdot
(\mathrm{hi}/\mathrm{lo})^q)$, the barrier at $\ln(\mathrm{hi})$, so the log-gap is
$\Delta=(1-q)\ln(\mathrm{hi}/\mathrm{lo})$; an onset is a noise excursion over the
barrier, rate $h(t)=e^{-d(t)^2/2}$ with $d(t)=(\Delta-\ell(t))/\sigma$, where
$\ell(t)$ is the circadian rise of the log-load (piecewise linear, zero-mean over the
day); the pre-exponential cancels in all ratios. This one model
yields, unfitted: the late-wake onset share (F2a: $0.856$ predicted, $0.885$ measured), the swing-response curve,
the mid-scale fork (F5), the burst-trigger share (F8a), the wake-lengthening ratio from
the deterministic switch (F4a, F6a), the affine duration law (F7, F8b). A rank
correlation across eight bins has error $1/\sqrt{7}=0.38$; the fix is per-set slopes with
attenuation $\rho_{\mathrm{set}}=\rho_{\mathrm{pool}}/\sqrt{1+\lambda}$ --- in the real
data $\lambda=33.8$ turned an MDE of $0.94$ into $0.076$ and closed four of five parts.

\subsection{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}
(i) \emph{Frozen monolith}: the regression suite requires the set sampling,
band arithmetic, and \code{simulate} to match the frozen monolith bitwise on pinned
configurations; cross-machine comparison uses $10^{-12}$ with a machine fingerprint
(measured worst $3\times10^{-15}$). (ii) \emph{Fused kernel}: assembled from the
reference source by textual substitution with uniqueness checks (an edit breaks the
assembly, never diverges silently); verified on all 67 output fields over nine pinned
configurations (counting bitwise; worst continuous deviation $9.5\times10^{-15}$ vs.\
$10^{-12}$) and by diffing all verdicts; $3.3\times$ wall-clock gain. (iii) \emph{Step
size}: continuous means converge to $0.5\,\%$ at $M=800$; counting quantities do not ---
the Richardson limit is $2.0\,\%$ above the value at $M=1600$ --- so counting thresholds
finer than $\approx3\,\%$ are not trusted, stated rather than hidden.

\subsection{Ten statistical traps}\label{sec:traps}
Each once produced a false conclusion in our hands; each is closed by a code check:
(1) median over sets of a rare-event share is degenerate; (2) the share of short gaps
$\equiv1+\mathrm{CV}^2$ of set rates --- physics-free; (3) sign-test direction is a
mandatory argument; (4) zero error by construction in self-normalised columns;
(5) a saturating measured quantity drowns real effects --- measure the rate, not the
ever-share; (6) an equivalence tolerance wider than the curve's own range is
unfalsifiable; (7) the mean of a bimodal population ($D>0.9$ vs.\ $D<0.01$) is the share
of broken sets, not a state; (8) a threshold must be \emph{attainable} --- an exact permutation
test over three points has a floor of $1/3!=0.167$, so a requirement of $p\le0.05$ cannot be met
by any behaviour of the model, and a perfectly monotone result ($\rho=+1.000$) was once recorded
as failed; (9) a comparison point taken as the nearest element of a grid that does not bracket the
target answers a question about the grid's edge, and a nearest element exists for every grid, so
the defect is silent; (10) the range of $n$ noisy values is itself random, so any threshold on
spread must be compared with its own expected range before it is written down.

\subsection{Established}\label{sec:survived}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \emph{Duration is set by defence memory, not repair capacity.} 99.8\,\% of the
within-set duration variance is explained by load history, 0.2\,\% by repair (exact
decomposition). The law is affine in the defence memory
$\tau_b$ (the slow stage of the two-stage chain):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dur}
\text{duration}=1.28+0.97\,\tau_b\ \text{(days)},
\end{equation}
fitted below 100\,h, checked at an out-of-fit 150\,h; a shared-offset saturating form
is rejected (F16b) though the quantitative out-of-fit prediction missed its
$2\,\mathrm{SE}$ band (F16a). Passive repair misses the two-day target by $2$--$10\times$
(the needed memory, $\approx12$\,h, is several times shorter than every measured
second-stage value, 24--88\,h).
\item \emph{Slow defence memory makes the pre-episode precursor:} removing it lowers
the precursor in 107 of 174 sets, $p=0.0015$; slow load noise is unnecessary.
\item \emph{Three phases, not two.} A band of intact cells \emph{more} vulnerable than
their collapsed neighbours: $0.00732\pm0.00042$ of the population ($17\sigma$;
$\approx8.5\,\%$ on a fine grid); exactly zero with frozen defence; $1.82\times$ wider
under priming. Population-level hormesis, a model prediction, not a counting artefact.
\item \emph{Priming (F19) reaches the clinical target:} the one-sided $95\,\%$ upper bound on
the median episode duration is $1.51$\,d, inside the two-day target; the paired sign test gives
$p=4.0\times10^{-43}$ in the written direction and $p=9.3\times10^{-13}$ for the reversed
direction, and label permutation calibrates the criterion at $0.049$; all four parts pass,
replicated on 2000 sets. Unfitted: ceiling
$\times3$ measured, $\tau_p$ scanned; accelerating the first stage gives $-1$ to
$-5\,\%$ vs.\ $-52\,\%$ for amplification, as predicted. Mechanism: priming raises the
\emph{top} of the window, not repair speed.
\item \emph{Asymmetric defence (F23):} scanning $r=0.33,0.5,1,2,3$ predicts
$w=0.441,0.544,0.705,0.827,0.878$ by~\eqref{eq:wr}; measured defence levels
$0.0575,0.0644,0.0792,0.0958,0.1065$ --- order exact ($\rho=+1$, permutation
$p=0.0083$). Damaged share falls $0.486\to0.457$ (Cochran--Armitage trend statistic $1.67$ in the written
direction); duration $7.74\to6.88$\,d.
Equal rise/decay constants were a \emph{choice of regime}, not a simplification; $r$
must be a model parameter unless measurement fixes it.
\item \emph{Capacity loop (F29) converts frequent short episodes into rare long ones:}
duration $13.1\to25.6$\,d while the onset rate \emph{falls} $0.042\to0.033$ per
at-risk set-day; episodicity $0.75$ of the open model; the broken state is held after
release, monotone in release depth ($\rho=+1.000$, $p=0.0083$); progression exceeds a
capacity-matched static control by $+0.0231$ vs.\ MDE $0.0096$, replicated as
$+0.0223$, $+0.0211$ on two further seeds. The one failing part: the \emph{shape} of risk
in time is unchanged (risk trend $1.94$ vs.\ threshold $2.23$, F29c).
\item \emph{Rhythm: a capture staircase} with rational rotation numbers and alternating
cycle lengths on fractional steps (section~\ref{sec:staircase}).
\item \emph{$J$ enters only as effective spread of resilience;} the $J\to\infty$ limit
exists with $1/J$ corrections (section~\ref{sec:J}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Refuted}\label{sec:failed}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Grace-type load loop (F26, F27):} irreversibility latches at loop strength
$1.11$ while episodes extinguish at $0.18$ --- a sixfold structural gap, robust to a
gentler protocol; no window with chronicity and episodicity together. The open model
releases all broken sets ($0.9936\to0.0000$), the loop releases $0.24\,\%$. Reason:
load is per-set, so the loop moves the whole population.
\item \emph{Capacity loop:} level and duration change, the shape of risk in time does
not (above).
\item \emph{Bursty load does not beat smooth load (F24):} onset rate falls 40\,\% as
correlation time grows at fixed mean and variance; the ever-damaged share saturates.
\item \emph{Serotonin burst (F8):} $26\,\%$ of onsets follow a burst, against an analytic
expectation of $43\,\%$ and a pre-registered threshold of $80\,\%$ --- a volume knob, not a
trigger; no episode property changes.
\item \emph{Daily swing (F2b):} smooth $1.5\times$ growth, no gate; gradualness is from
spread, not network (F5, observation \emph{below} the analytic fork).
\item \emph{The episode does not remember its depth (F10a):} duration matches
time-above-window (rank correlation $0.94$ old / $0.89$ current model); tail
$\approx6\,\%$ of the episode.
\item \emph{Band shape} $\varphi(\Phi^{-1}(1-D))$ refuted twice with opposite signs
(F25d,e) --- width is not a function of $D$.
\item \emph{$J$-substitution check (F22b) failed:} the effective spread explains the
measured drop but does not close the question (candidates: $1/J$ mean-dispersion,
discreteness of $D$ in the sleep feedback).
\item Several precursor-slope parts were unfalsifiable at the chosen grid resolution
(MDE larger than the written threshold).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Selected mechanisms}\label{sec:selected}
\paragraph{The staircase (F20).}\label{sec:staircase}
The deterministic switch under 24\,h forcing, with
damage inhibiting the sleep nucleus: the rotation number is pinned at rationals and
jumps:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stair}
\begin{gathered}
1{:}1\to5{:}4\to3{:}2\to2{:}1\to3{:}1\to4{:}1\to\dots\to9{:}1\\
\to\text{no sleep},
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
transitions at $kD\approx4.77$; $4.78$; $4.79$; $5.09$; $5.16$\,mV; five steps
(5:1--9:1) within $5.172$--$5.178$\,mV. A fine run: 52 steps in $0.9$\,mV, 34 with
alternating cycle lengths (patterns $25,24,24,47$\,h at $4.772$; $47,25$ at $4.779$;
$46,26,24$ at $4.783$); widths $0.3$\,mV (2:1), $8\times10^{-3}$ (3:2),
$5\times10^{-6}$ (11:8). Capture staircase (Arnold tongues), \emph{not} a
period-doubling cascade: the period adds one skipped night; the discriminator is a
capture on a non-power-of-two period (found). Limitation is calibration: $kD\le k$
identically, so at $k=2$\,mV the first step ($4.77$\,mV) is unreachable at any damage;
at $k=5$\,mV the model reaches it ($kD=4.93$, $D=0.999$).

\paragraph{Cell count $J$ (F13, F22).}\label{sec:J}
Three paths: (1) the tail of the vulnerability distribution --- closed by the
fixed-edge grid (at $J=32$ identical to the grid of earlier versions to the last digit); (2)
quantisation of the episode threshold ($0.625$ at $J=8$ vs.\ $0.516$ at $J=64$) ---
bypassed via the continuous variable; (3) the realised spread (section~\ref{sec:pop}):
11\,\% in effective $\sigma_v$ between $J=16$ and $64$, at log-sensitivity $\approx2.5$
$\Rightarrow$ $\approx25\,\%$ in measured quantities --- exactly the range F13 found at
2000 sets. A discretisation can preserve mean and edges exactly and still miss the
variance ($O(1/J)$): comparing runs at different $J$ compares different models.

\paragraph{The capacity loop (F28, F29).} The first edition collapsed on its own
controls: ``no increment with the loop open'' is false --- a bistable switch never
releases a broken set, so the broken share rises on its own; the control passed at
small $n$ and failed as data accumulated. F29 therefore contains \emph{no test against
zero}: all claims are matched differences at equal damage level (section~\ref{sec:lessons}).

\paragraph{The band (F25).} On a 600-node grid: frozen defence $\Rightarrow$ exact
monotonicity (zero violations); live defence breaks order on $11.9\,\%$ of steps, worst
violation $0.973$ (full reversal); priming amplifies, does not cause ($A=0$:
$0.0184$). Consequence: the closed-form damaged fraction $1-F_v(v^*)$ --- $F_v$ the cumulative
distribution of the load multiplier, $v^*$ its threshold value --- is illegal under
live defence; any reduction needs an explicit three-configuration boundary layer.

\section{Methodological lessons}\label{sec:lessons}
In a model with absorbing states and a heterogeneous population, \emph{no absolute null
exists for any accumulation measure}: the damaged fraction rises on its own (bistable
sets do not release) and the risk falls on its own (the vulnerable are absorbed
first). Two of our controls asserted these baselines were zero; both were false and
both \emph{passed at small sample and failed as data accumulated} --- a false null is
passed by lack of power, since the threshold shrinks as $1/\sqrt{n}$ while the baseline
is fixed. Every claim must be a matched difference between arms at the same damage
level. The ten traps (section~\ref{sec:traps}) are this lesson in ten costumes; the tenth cost
us directly: $\mathbb{E}[\text{range}]=1.693\,r$ for $n=3$, so a ``spread below 10\,\%''
threshold was meaningless at $r=12\,\%$ and became testable only at 2000 sets --- where it
failed and exposed the third path of $J$.

\section{Scope and open questions}\label{sec:scope}
The model has no absorption-free stationary state: it is valid in windows short
compared with the accumulation time --- a model of the \emph{generation and termination
of an episode}, not of the course of illness. Everything measured here is moreover conditional on
the bistability premise: a parameter set enters the study only if its cell already sits inside the
window, and whether real PV cells do is \emph{not} established by this work. The measured cell
lines lie outside it (section~\ref{sec:core}) and entering requires repair weakened
$1.6$--$22$-fold; that weakening is the hypothesis under test, not a result of the tests. The capacity loop is the only mechanism
tested that makes progression appear, and it does so by lengthening episodes, not by
multiplying them. Open: (i) progression on illness scale needs a component that does
not fully return --- deliberately not added: a mechanism introduced to produce its
target result is not a test; the falsifying rule comes first; (ii) regions coupled
through narrow bottlenecks vs.\ the per-set homogeneity that killed the load loop;
(iii) rhythm breakdown unreachable at $k=2$\,mV vs.\ first step $4.77$\,mV ---
miscalibration or another path; (iv) the band width is set by something third (likely
the time near the tipping point) --- testable against the mid-scale residence time,
must agree on both scans or it is withdrawn; (v) the third slow variable behind the
precursor (population filling has no own $\tau$); (vi) the free term of~\eqref{eq:dur}
($1.3$\,d at zero memory) is unexplained by $x$; (vii) peroxiredoxin kinetics are from
cell lines, not PV cells; (viii) dopamine and symptoms are outside the model by design.
